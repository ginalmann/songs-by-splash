Tell me your story

What should the song remember?

The best songs aren't built from perfect words. They're built from the little details that make your story yours.

Tell me about things like:

  • Who they are to you — and what makes your relationship special.
  • How you met — or a moment that changed everything.
  • Favorite memories — trips, places, traditions, or ordinary moments you still think about.
  • The little things — phrases they say, habits, quirks, or inside jokes.
  • Important milestones — weddings, children, moves, accomplishments, or challenges you've shared.
  • What you love about them — the qualities you never want them to forget.
  • A meaningful place — a street, restaurant, city, house, or somewhere only the two of you understand.
  • The feeling you want to capture — romantic, funny, nostalgic, joyful, emotional, soulful, or bittersweet.
  • Anything that absolutely belongs in the song — names, dates, sayings, or a memory that has to make the lyrics.

Don't worry about making it sound like a song. That's my part. Just tell me the story.

Custom song FAQ

Questions about personalized songs.

Clear answers about turning a real story into a song.

Can I turn my story into a custom song?

Yes. Songs by Splash creates personalized songs from the people, memories, places, sayings, inside jokes and moments that matter to you.

What information do I need for a personalized song?

You do not need to write lyrics. Share who the song is for, meaningful memories, places, milestones, sayings, inside jokes and the feeling you want the song to capture. I shape those details into the song.

Can you create a custom wedding song or first-dance song?

Yes. A custom wedding song can be built from your real relationship story—how you met, favorite memories, meaningful places, humor, milestones and what you want your partner or guests to feel. Learn about custom wedding songs →

Can I choose the music style?

Yes. You can share the genre, mood, tempo, vocal feel or general musical direction you love. If you are unsure, you can choose “Surprise Me.”

Can I give a custom song as a gift?

Absolutely. Personalized songs can be created for weddings, anniversaries, birthdays, friendships, parents, family milestones, memorials and meaningful occasions when an ordinary gift does not feel personal enough.

Do I need to know anything about songwriting?

No. Just tell me the story. I find the hook, shape the lyrics and develop the musical direction.
