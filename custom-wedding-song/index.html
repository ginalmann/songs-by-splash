Wedding song FAQ

Questions couples ask.

Can you make a custom wedding song from our love story?

Yes. Share how you met, meaningful memories, places, inside jokes, milestones and what you want the song to say. I shape those details into a personalized wedding song.

Can the song be used for our first dance?

Yes. Tell me the feeling and general musical direction you want, and the song can be developed with a first-dance moment in mind.

Do we have to write the lyrics ourselves?

No. You provide the story and details. I develop the lyrics, hook, structure and musical direction.

Can we choose the genre?

Yes. You can request a broad direction such as R&B, soul, pop, country, rock, acoustic, indie or another style, or ask me to recommend what fits your story.
